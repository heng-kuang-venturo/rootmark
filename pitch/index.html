Pitch deck

Rootmark

Grow a tree for every chapter of life — fruit, cherry blossom, maple, and more on local farms, watched year by year.

Problem

Milestones deserve more than flowers that wilt.

  • People want living gifts for births, anniversaries, memorials — but few have land, time, or know-how.
  • Moving, renting, and apartment life make backyard trees fragile promises.
  • Carbon offsets feel abstract; local farms need stable revenue beyond seasonal tourists.

Solution

A milestone tree hosted by a farm near you.

PlantGift or subscribe; we plant your tree on a partner farm or orchard.
WatchFarm photos across years and seasons — growth stages, blooms, and harvest notes when it’s a fruit tree.
ShareVisit anytime; for fruit trees, pick or donate harvest to the farm and community.

Coverage: fruit trees (harvest highlight) · cherry blossoms & flower trees · maple & ornamentals.

Business model

Three revenue rails

Gift planting$180–$320 one-time: tree + first-year care + digital timeline + gift narrative.
Care subscription$8–$15/mo after year one: photos, agronomy notes, visit windows, harvest options.
Farm partnershipHost fee per tree/year + optional visitor day packages; farms keep donated fruit value.

Operations

Farm network + monthly photo logistics.

  • Onboard orchards with spare row capacity; standardized planting & labeling kits.
  • Farms upload monthly photos via mobile web; Rootmark tags phenology tips.
  • Visit booking windows coordinated with farms; harvest opt-in or donate-by-default.
  • Carbon story tied to living trees + soil practices — no phantom offsets.
