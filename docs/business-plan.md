# Rootmark — Business plan & GTM strategy

## 1. Problem
People want to honor life milestones with something living and lasting. Flowers fade; digital cards disappear. Planting a tree at home fails for renters, movers, and anyone without horticultural confidence. Meanwhile, “plant a tree” offsets often feel unverifiable, and local farms struggle to monetize spare orchard capacity outside peak U-pick weekends.

## 2. Solution
**Rootmark** sells milestone trees — **fruit trees as a highlight**, plus ornamentals such as **cherry blossoms** and **maples** — that are **planted and cared for on partner farms** near the customer. Owners get:
1. A gift narrative tied to a real tree and farm.
2. A **monitoring website** with multi-year / seasonal farm-uploaded photos and educational notes.
3. Options to visit; for fruit trees, harvest or **donate fruit** to the farm/community.
4. A climate story grounded in living biomass and farm stewardship—not abstract credits alone.

## 3. Market
### Customer segments
- Consumer gifters (baby, wedding, anniversary, memorial)
- Registry & event planners
- Corporate recognition / employee milestones
- Faith & hospice memorial programs (later)

### TAM / SAM / SOM (planning estimates — clearly labeled)
| Layer | Estimate | Assumption |
|-------|----------|------------|
| TAM | ~$85–120B | Global celebratory/experiential gifting + memorial plantings + tree/offset adjacency (order-of-magnitude blend) |
| SAM | ~$8–14B | US + Western Europe premium experiential gifts + registry-adjacent spend + agritourism-capable households near orchards |
| SOM | ~$40–90M over ~5 years | Density in ~20 US metros + 2 EU pilots; 150–300k cumulative trees; mix of gift ASP ~$240 and subscriptions |

Refine with primary interviews and registry partner data before fundraising materials harden.

## 4. Product
- **Gift SKU:** fruit or ornamental variety + planting + year-1 care + digital timeline + optional print card.
- **Subscription:** ongoing photos, education, visit windows, harvest coordination.
- **Farm console:** mobile upload, tree IDs, stipend dashboard.
- **Monitor mock:** see `monitor.html` for UX concept.

## 5. Operations
1. **Farm network:** recruit farms/orchards with spare rows; standardize planting kits, labels, photo angles.
2. **Planting seasons:** spring/fall batches; off-season gift sales with scheduled plant dates.
3. **Monthly photo ops:** farm staff photograph labeled trees; Rootmark QA + educational captions.
4. **Logistics:** no tree shipping to customers; visitors travel to farms; fruit donate-in-place by default.
5. **Risk:** disease, weather, farm churn — mitigated via multi-tree buffer rows and replacement policy.

## 6. Unit economics (sketch)
Illustrative Year-1 gift tree at **$240 ASP**:
- Sapling + plant labor: $35–55
- Farm host stipend: $40–60
- Photo ops + QA: $18–28
- Payments + packaging: $20–30
- **Contribution margin:** ~$70–120 before CAC

Subscription **$8–15/mo** with high gross margin after year one. Target CAC $40–80 via registries and farm events; attach rate goal ≥45%.

- Multi-region playbook
- Memorial / institutional channels
- Optimize subscription attach and farm NPS

## 9. Milestones
- [ ] 3 farms live with SLA for monthly photos
- [ ] 500 trees planted; ≥40% subscription attach
- [ ] Registry partner LOI
- [ ] Replacement rate &lt;5% year one
- [ ] Documented carbon & education methodology

## 10. Risks & mitigations
- **Farm reliability** → multi-farm redundancy, stipend tied to photo SLA
- **Seasonality** → sell gifts year-round against plant windows
- **Trust** → transparent tree IDs, GPS/farm pages, photo timestamps
- **Regulation** → food donation rules handled farm-side; Rootmark facilitates intent
