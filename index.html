How it works

A living gift that keeps growing.

You mark the moment. We plant and care for a tree near you — fruit for harvest, or ornamental favorites like cherry and maple. You watch it grow across the years.

Choose a milestone

Birth, wedding, memorial, graduation — pick a fruit tree, cherry blossom, maple, or another variety that fits the story.

We plant on a partner farm

Your tree lives with growers who already know soil, pruning, and care. No backyard stress. No moving panic.

Follow monthly photos

A private web timeline shows blossoms, canopy, fruit set, and seasonal cues — education baked into every year.

Why Rootmark

Sentiment, seasons, and climate — one root system.

  • Honor life without the upkeepFarms host and care for your tree. Visit when you want; we handle the rest.
  • Fruit trees as a highlightHarvest education, pick windows, and the option to donate fruit to the farm and community.
  • Ornamentals tooCherry blossoms, maples, and other living markers for milestones that aren’t about harvest.
  • Real trees, real carbonEach planting adds living biomass and soil stewardship through partner farms.

Local farms

Connected to growers near you.

Rootmark is a farm network, not a nursery shipment. Partner farms and orchards earn hosting fees, welcome visitors, and — for fruit trees — keep harvest circulating locally.

Tree coverage

More than fruit trees.

Fruit trees are our harvest highlight — but Rootmark also plants ornamental and flower trees for milestones that need beauty, shade, or remembrance.

  • Fruit treesPeach, apple, and more — learn bloom and harvest timing; pick or donate fruit to the farm and community.
  • Cherry blossoms & flower treesSeasonal spectacle for births, weddings, and spring celebrations.
  • Maple & ornamentalsColor, canopy, and quiet markers for memorials and anniversaries.
