How it works

A living gift that keeps growing.

You mark the moment. We plant and care for a tree near you — fruit, flowering, or a long-lived companion. You watch it grow across the years.

Choose a milestone

Birth, wedding, memorial, graduation — pick a fruit tree, flowering tree, or long-lived companion that fits the story.

We plant on a partner farm

Your tree lives with growers who already know soil, pruning, and care. No backyard stress. No moving panic.

Follow monthly photos

A private web timeline shows blossoms, canopy, fruit set, and seasonal cues — education baked into every year.

Why Rootmark

Sentiment, seasons, and climate — one root system.

  • Honor life without the upkeepFarms host and care for your tree. Visit when you want; we handle the rest.
  • Fruit treesPeach, apple, pear — harvest education, pick windows, and donate-to-farm options.
  • Flowering & companion treesCherry blossom, magnolia, oak, maple, and more for celebration and lifelong markers.
  • Real trees, real carbonEach planting adds living biomass and soil stewardship through partner farms.

Local farms

Connected to growers near you.

Rootmark is a farm network, not a nursery shipment. Partner farms and orchards earn hosting fees, welcome visitors, and help keep fruit harvests circulating locally.
